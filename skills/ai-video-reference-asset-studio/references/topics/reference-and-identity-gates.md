# Reference And Identity Gates

Current user intent and authorization outrank an approved original source; the original source outranks derivatives; visible reference evidence outranks descriptive reconstruction; verified platform behavior outranks untested model advice; style never overrides identity, product truth, or geometry.

- A new fictional character may begin from text. Its root candidate must satisfy the selected delivery profile before approval. Under `higgsfield-three-panel`, the complete sheet itself is the root candidate; a single-view calibration image is not the root deliverable.
- A recognizable person requires an authorized source.
- Every angle, wardrobe, expression, or state derivative points directly to the original approved base. Generate a complete new image from that root; do not edit or chain through the latest derivative.
- A multi-view sheet may be the first root candidate when the selected delivery profile defines the sheet as the identity asset. Under `higgsfield-three-panel`, panel authority is asymmetric: left controls front body/wardrobe without a face, center controls the back without facial features, and right alone controls face and expression identity. Once approved, the sheet is the sole identity source for later angle, wardrobe, expression, action, or state derivatives.
- An environment master proves only visible topology. Hidden/reverse areas need overlapping evidence, a spatial control board, or an upstream decision.
- Props used in contact, opening, breaking, inserting, or handoff need visible structure, scale, and separate states.
- A style image controls light, palette, material rendering, grain, contrast, or capture character only. Prefer a subject-neutral style study when an identifiable subject would contaminate downstream identity.
